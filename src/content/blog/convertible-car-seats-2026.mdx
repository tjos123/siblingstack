## The two-seats-at-once consideration this category doesn't usually address

If you're buying for two kids close in age, [check whether your chosen seats share the same LATCH/attachment system](/blog/car-seat-two-different-sizes), even across two different models — it makes swapping a seat between cars, or reinstalling after cleaning, considerably less of an ordeal when you're not relearning a new system each time. It's also worth [physically testing that your two chosen seats actually fit side by side](/blog/double-stroller-roundup) in your specific back seat before buying both — manufacturer "fits 3 across" claims are optimistic more often than not.

:::checklist
📋 Buyer's Action Checklist for Two Convertible Seats
-Verify NHTSA recall status for all candidate seats prior to purchase.
-Measure your vehicle's backseat width and check seat belt / LATCH anchor placement.
-Confirm rear-facing weight limits match your growth-timeline goals.
-Ensure both seats use compatible latching mechanisms if you swap vehicles frequently.
-Test side-by-side fit before removing tags or disposing of packaging.
:::
