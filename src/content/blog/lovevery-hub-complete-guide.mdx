# The Complete SiblingStack Guide to Lovevery for Two Under Two

Twenty-three guides, built around one real question — is this worth it for a household with two kids close in age — answered from every angle that question actually comes up in: the core financial math, gift-giving, registry planning, and the family situations that don't fit the standard two-under-two mold. Start with the section that matches why you're actually here.

**Quick answer, if you only read one thing:** across a fully corrected, box-by-box financial audit, a Lovevery subscription saves a two-under-two household roughly 32% versus running two independent subscriptions — well below the "half the cost" marketing claim, but a real, defensible number once you account for which specific items actually survive to a second child. The full reasoning, with every number shown, is in our [core master guide](https://www.siblingstack.com/blog/lovevery-two-under-two-box-by-box-audit) below.

## Where Should I Start?

**I want the short version of the whole financial case.** Start with the [core master guide](https://www.siblingstack.com/blog/lovevery-two-under-two-box-by-box-audit) — everything else in this cluster either builds on it or answers a narrower question it doesn't cover.

**I'm not the parent — I'm buying this as a gift, or helping plan a registry.** Skip straight to [Section 2](#2-buying-this-as-a-gift) below.

**My family situation doesn't match the standard "two kids, one household, close in age" setup this content usually assumes.** Go to [Section 3](#3-if-your-familys-situation-is-a-little-different) — it covers twins, blended families, co-parenting across two homes, prematurity, adoption and foster placement, daycare, and international buyers.

**I already have a subscription running and need help managing it.** [Section 4](#4-already-subscribed-managing-maintaining-and-eventually-exiting) covers storage, cleaning, individual item purchases, resale, and eventually winding it all down.
