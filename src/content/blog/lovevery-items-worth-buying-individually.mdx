## 1. The Object Permanence Box (Hide & Find Drop Box)

**From:** The Inspector Play Kit (7-8 months)
**New-purchase status:** Confirmed standalone, sold directly and through retail partners including Target and Babylist.

This is the classic Montessori object permanence box — a small wooden drawer-and-ball unit where a baby drops a ball into a slot, watches it disappear, and opens a drawer to retrieve it, building an early understanding that objects continue to exist even when out of sight. It's officially listed at 5.28" x 5.71" x 5.71", about 1.2 pounds, made from birch and plywood with FSC-certified sourcing and water-based, non-toxic finishes, and ships as a two-piece set (box plus one ball).

**Confirmed current pricing:** Standalone new pricing runs $24.99-$28.96 depending on retailer — Target and Babylist list it at the lower end, Walmart slightly higher. Customer ratings on the item specifically run strong (4.9 out of 5 across available reviews). Used units on secondhand marketplaces have been observed around $22-23.

**Why it's worth buying individually:** This is one of the most consistently praised single items across Lovevery's entire early lineup, and per our durability audit, object permanence boxes generally hold up extremely well to repeated use — there's very little that can break on a simple drawer-and-ball mechanism. If you're not subscribing to the full Inspector kit but want this one specific, well-regarded developmental toy, the confirmed standalone availability makes it one of the easiest "just buy the one thing" decisions in this whole guide.
