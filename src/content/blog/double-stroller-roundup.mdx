Most "best double stroller" roundups are written for twins — same size, same seat needs. If you've got a newborn and a toddler instead, [the stroller has a harder job](/blog/double-stroller-close-in-age): one seat needs a near-flat recline, the other needs to fit a toddler who wants to face forward and watch the world.

*Prices, stock, and exact specs shift constantly — treat the numbers below as a starting point and confirm current details on the retailer page before you buy.*

## What to check before buying any of these

:::checklist
What to Check Before Buying Any Double Stroller
-[Per-seat weight limit, not the combined total](/blog/double-stroller-close-in-age) — marketing often leads with a combined number that doesn't tell you what either individual seat can hold.
-Whether the infant seat attaches directly or needs an adapter — an adapter is an extra cost and an extra thing to lose.
-Your actual trunk space and front door width, measured — side-by-side doubles are wider than they look in product photos.
:::
