✅ What Matters Most with Two Kids Close in Age
-**Tray & seat-pad cleanability:** Outweighs almost every other feature — you'll be cleaning these multiple times a day.
-**Verify the real footprint:** Check the actual width against your dining space before buying — dual units can take up significant floor space.
-**Look for long-term conversion:** Chairs that transition to toddler boosters or standalone furniture mean you don't need double storage.
-**Dishwasher-safe parts:** Removable tray inserts save essential hours across hundreds of weekly meal cleanups.
