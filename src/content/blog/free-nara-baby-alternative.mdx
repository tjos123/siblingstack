## What changed with Nara Baby?

Nara was long known as a clean, ad-free tracker with a generous free version. Based on Nara's FAQ as reported by several comparison sites, new users now get a **7-day trial**, after which the app costs **$9.99 per month or $99.99 for a lifetime plan**. Without a plan, the app becomes read-only: you can view and export your log but not add new entries.

For many families that's fair value, and Nara remains a polished app. But if you want a tracker that stays free through the newborn months and beyond, it's worth looking at alternatives.
